# Higgsfield Media Production

Turn a creative brief into a practical Higgsfield workflow. Adapt to the user's domain, language, audience, format, editor, and production assistant. Do not require a particular brand, video length, visual style, coding stack, or assistant model.

## Identify the task and its authorization

Distinguish research, planning, quoting, generation, editing, and checking existing jobs. Research or a production plan does not itself request a generation, even when the user has authorized spending in other work.

Carry forward the current project's explicit authorization: which outputs may consume credits, which benefits may be used, any batch or total ceiling, and existing reference assets. Do not turn one project's restrictions or balances into defaults for other projects. A skill invocation is not a grant to buy credits, change a subscription, or publish media.

Ask only for missing information that affects the next dependent action. Continue useful independent planning while the answer is pending. Do not ask again for an unchanged, already authorized free/Unlimited choice; still honor confirmation gates actually imposed by the execution tool.

For account benefits, budget boundaries, preflight, or retries, read [spending-and-recovery.md](references/spending-and-recovery.md).

## Discover capabilities and benefits separately

Use the connected Higgsfield tools to discover current models, required media, parameters, prices, and account benefits. Discover tools by capability instead of assuming a host-specific namespace. Common capabilities are balance, model list/search/get, cost estimation, generation, and job status.

Inspect only the model or shortlist relevant to the task. A broad market survey may justify pagination through the catalog; a single asset usually does not. Use current official Higgsfield documentation to resolve gaps or web-specific features. Do not use a saved catalog as proof of present availability or entitlement.

Treat these as separate facts:

- A model supports Unlimited.
- This account has an active benefit for the requested configuration and channel.
- A particular free-generation counter applies to this product and its inputs.
- A submission actually completed without consuming credits.

Missing entitlement data means unverified, not unavailable. Do not conclude that all variants, resolutions, formats, or tools are covered by a model's badge. Website, connector, API, Canvas, and other execution surfaces may have different coverage.

Prefer the connected tool when it can complete the authorized operation. If only the website exposes the relevant benefit, explain that distinction and use supported browser tools when available. If that path is unavailable, preserve the plan or prepared assets and report the specific limitation; do not silently switch to a paid channel.

## Choose the workflow by the deliverable

Read [model-selection.md](references/model-selection.md) when comparing models or choosing between generation, editing, vectorization, motion transfer, and other operations.

Select against the required result, not the newest model name. Consider reference fidelity, typography, transparency, editable structure, frame control, performance, audio, output format, and cost per accepted result. Explain any recommendation as a fit assessment unless actual comparative generations were reviewed.

If the user names a model, preset, or slash command, preserve that choice and resolve its current instructions before selecting a substitute. Follow the installed Higgsfield command workflow where available. Do not automatically start advertising, avatar, cinematic, or editor pipelines from a general media request.

The final deliverable may be a generated image, complete video, audio, editable asset kit, or a reference for another tool. Do not force every request into code-based animation. When the task needs exact interfaces, typography, timing, or reusable components, allocate those parts to deterministic authoring and use generation where it adds value.

For layered assets, transitions, or collaboration with a motion/coding assistant, read [motion-handoff.md](references/motion-handoff.md).

## Execute an authorized generation

Use the discovered schema and the applicable benefit or cost preflight. Preserve the user's model, inputs, scope, count, output destination, and spending allocation. A quote does not establish that required media are present or that a job has been submitted.

Start at the scale needed to validate the uncertain part of the task. Avoid imposing a fixed batch size, resolution, or clip duration: use the smallest suitable supported configuration, unless the user requested a final-quality output or an exact setting.

Keep accepted job IDs and follow them through supported status tools. Follow the tool's polling and display rules. An uncertain submission must be reconciled before retrying; do not create another potentially paid job just to recover a missing response or display.

## Verify and deliver

Inspect the actual result against the brief: required objects and identity, composition, text, transparency, motion continuity, frame endpoints, and audio or timing when relevant. An exported SVG may need grouping before animation; a generated video does not supply editable layers, exact curves, or implementation code.

Report what was planned, quoted, submitted, failed, or completed. Show completed media through the supported native display or an accessible result artifact. For handoffs, identify the approved assets, their role, and the implementation details the next tool needs.

Record model and configuration, source/reference roles, benefit channel, quoted cost, reported actual cost when available, job IDs, and selected result. Keep account identifiers, private references, credentials, and project-specific balances out of reusable or public skill packages.
